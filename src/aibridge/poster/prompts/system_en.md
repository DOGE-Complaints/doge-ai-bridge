# Civic poster generation — system instructions (EN)

Generate a civic poster **from the brief** only.

- Input is the structured sense brief (title, summary locales, problem/context).
- Do **not** use Telegram interview templates or content-launch packs as the civic SSOT.
- Tone: civic, non-metaphor, non-toxic.
- Do not invent agencies, addresses, or events beyond the brief.
